Questions, answered

Something else? Message us on WhatsApp, we reply the same day. Every detail is also on the TeXcela facts page.

How much does it cost?
₹35,000 for the full 16 weeks, or ₹30,000 if you score Ready on the free skill check. You can pay in parts. There are no loans and no hidden extras.
When does the first batch start?
Monday, 11 January 2027. Take the free skill check to reserve your place.
Do I need to know coding already?
You need basic programming in any language; this is not a course for complete beginners. The skill check has no pass mark. If you have gaps, a short foundations bridge gets you ready first. If you need more time, we give you a prep path and a re-check for the next batch.
Can I do it alongside college or a job?
Yes. Live classes are on Monday and Wednesday evenings and build labs on Saturdays. Plan for about 12 to 14 hours a week.
Do you guarantee a job?
No, and be careful of anyone who does. We prove what you can do, prepare you for interviews and introduce job-ready learners to Xcela's client companies. See what we do promise.
Can I pay in parts, and what if I leave?
Yes, in instalments on our course platform, with all dates shown before you pay. We don't arrange loans or auto-debits. If you leave: a full refund within 14 days of the batch start.
Isn't using AI cheating?
No. It's how teams work now. We score how well you prompt, review, test and explain AI-written code, and checkpoint tests are taken without AI, so the work is still yours.
What is the Readiness Profile?
A profile of ten skills companies hire for, showing the level you reached in each and the work behind every score. It is checked by tests, AI review and people, and set with Xcela recruiters.
